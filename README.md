## 🕹️ About

Dimraeth is an open-world action RPG set in a handcrafted pixel-art fantasy world threatened by spreading corruption. Build an immortal hero, explore Mirkea, master elemental combat, forge relationships, and establish a stronghold alone or with up to seven other players. This repository contains an unofficial leaked Windows pre-release build that surfaced before the planned September 15, 2026 Early Access launch.

For players who want a deep fantasy action RPG with pixel-art exploration, build freedom, demanding combat, and unfinished early-build energy.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- ⚔️ **Souls-Lite Combat** — Fight through deliberate real-time battles where timing, positioning, and adaptation matter.
- 🔥 **Elemental Combat** — Combine elemental abilities and build powerful combat strategies.
- 🧬 **Hero Creation** — Shape your character across eight races, six classes, attributes, and a flexible skill tree.
- 🌲 **Open Pixel World** — Explore handcrafted regions, ancient ruins, forests, and dangerous hidden places.
- 🏰 **Stronghold Building** — Build farms, quarries, facilities, and a refuge that grows with your journey.
- 🤝 **Allies and Relationships** — Recruit companions, unlock personal quests, deepen relationships, and expand your settlement.
- 🛠️ **Crafting and Progression** — Collect resources, craft equipment, upgrade gear, and develop your own playstyle.
- 👥 **1–8 Player Co-op** — Play solo or join friends through seamless drop-in, drop-out online co-op.

 **Q: Will this affect my official game install or saves?**
